Your week,
written for you

Tell Trainr your goal, your experience and when you can train. It writes a full week around those answers, then builds the next one from what you actually lifted.

  • Weight loss, muscle gain, strength, endurance or general fitness
  • As many days as you have, sessions as long as you have
  • Injuries and problem areas are worked around, not ignored
  • Next week progresses from the sets you logged, not a template
Less time. Sore knee. No bench.

Bad day?
It bends.

Open Adjust today on any session and say what is going on. Trainr changes the workout, shows you the tradeoff, and applies it only when you say so.

  • Less time: keeps the most relevant work and cuts the rest
  • Equipment unavailable: finds a suitable alternative
  • Something hurts: pauses and decides what to do next
  • Or say it in your own words, read by a small model on your phone
At home or at the gym

Built for
what you’ve got

Pick the equipment you actually own, from a full gym to nothing at all. The week is written with that, and only that.

  • Bodyweight, dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, plates, bands and machines
  • Kilograms or pounds, with separate units for the plates in your gym
  • Change your kit or your days any time and start a fresh plan
Every set, every video

Know the move.
Beat last week.

Every exercise carries a video and labelled muscles. Last week’s numbers sit beside today’s, so you always know what to beat.

  • Log weight, reps and time for each set as you go
  • A built-in timer for planks, intervals and timed work
  • Tick off sets and watch the session fill in
  • Two clear ways to end: log the whole session, or save what you’ve done
Next week knows what you did

add up

Every finished week stays with you. Revisit any session, repeat a week that went well, or rewrite one that didn’t.

  • Weekly progress and the history of every past week
  • Repeat a week for free, as often as you like
  • Drag a session to another day when life gets in the way
Built on your phone. Stays on your phone.

No account.
No audience.

Trainr has no sign-up and no server of its own. Your profile, your training history and anything you type into Adjust today stay on the device.

Written on the deviceThe week is built by the app itself, from an exercise catalogue that ships inside it. Nothing you answer is sent anywhere to write it.
Read on the deviceThe free-text Adjust today note is read by a small language model running on your phone. The note never leaves it.
