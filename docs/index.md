# Trainr

Trainr builds your weekly workout plan on your phone, from your goals, the
equipment you actually have, and the days you can train. It tracks every set,
rep and weight, and builds next week from what you really lifted.

The plan is built on the device. There are no accounts, and your training
history stays on your phone.
