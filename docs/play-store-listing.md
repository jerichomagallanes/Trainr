# Play Store listing

The copy for the Store listing page in Play Console. Kept here so a change to
what the store says is reviewed like a change to what the app does.

Character limits are Play's own. The counts in brackets are the current text.

## App name (30 max)

```
Trainr: AI Workout Plans
```

[24] Unchanged. The two words people search for, "workout" and "AI", are both
in it, and the brand leads.

## Short description (80 max)

```
AI workout planner that builds your week around your goal, gear and schedule.
```

[77] This is the line under the icon in search results and the one Play weighs
most heavily after the title. It names the category (workout planner), the
differentiator (AI), and the promise (built around you) in one sentence.

## Full description (4000 max)

```
Trainr is an AI personal trainer that writes your workout plan for you.

Tell it your goal, your experience, where you train and what equipment you
have. Trainr generates a full week of workouts built around those answers, then
progresses next week from what you actually lifted.

No account. No sign-up. No subscription.

BUILT AROUND YOU
• A workout plan generated from your goal, experience and schedule
• Train at home, in the gym, or both
• Works with what you own: bodyweight, dumbbells, barbells, benches, kettlebells, resistance bands, pull-up bars, squat racks, cable and cardio machines
• Weight loss, muscle gain, strength, endurance or general fitness
• Injuries and problem areas are worked around, not ignored
• Choose how many days a week you train and how long each session runs

TRACK EVERY SET
• Log weight, reps and time for each set as you go
• Last week's numbers sit beside today's, so you know what to beat
• A built-in timer for planks, intervals and any timed exercise
• A video tutorial for every exercise, so you know the movement before you try it
• Tick off exercises as you finish them and watch the session fill in

YOUR WEEK, YOUR WAY
• Drag a session onto another weekday when life gets in the way
• Repeat a week that went well, or regenerate one that didn't
• Track completed weeks and revisit any past session
• Metric or imperial, with separate units for the plates in your gym

WHY TRAINR
Most workout apps hand you the same plan as everyone else. Trainr writes yours
from your answers, then rewrites it each week from your logged sets, the way a
coach would. It is a workout planner, a rep tracker and an exercise log in one,
and it works offline once your week is written.

PRIVACY
Your profile and training history stay on your phone. Trainr has no accounts
and no server of its own. Your answers are sent to Google Gemini only when a
plan is being generated. Full policy:
https://jerichomagallanes.github.io/Trainr/privacy-policy

Plans are generated by AI and are not medical advice. Check with a doctor
before starting a new programme, and stop if something hurts.
```

[2108] Well inside the limit. Play indexes the full description, so the terms
people actually search — workout planner, personal trainer, gym workout, home
workout, strength training, workout tracker, exercise log, rep tracker — each
appear once or twice in a sentence that reads normally. Repeating them harder
is what Play penalises.

The bullets exist because the tester report asked for scannable features, and
because only the first two lines show before "Read more": those two carry the
whole pitch on their own.

## What is deliberately not claimed

- No "unlimited" anything. Generation runs against a shared daily quota.
- No before-and-after or results claims. Play's health policy treats those as
  medical claims.
- No mention of wearables, community or streaks. The app has none.
