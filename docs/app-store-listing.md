# App Store listing

The copy for the App Store Connect product page. The Play listing in
`play-store-listing.md` is the source; this is the same pitch cut to Apple's
fields and limits. Counts in brackets are the current text.

## Description (4000 max)

```
Trainr builds your workout plan on your phone from your answers, and
progresses it from the sets you log.

Tell it your goal, your experience, where you train and what equipment you
have. Trainr builds a full week of workouts around those answers, then
progresses next week from what you actually lifted.

No account. No sign-up. Your first week is free, and Trainr Pro unlocks the
weeks after it.

BUILT AROUND YOU
• A workout plan built from your goal, experience and schedule
• Train at home, in the gym, or both
• Works with what you own: bodyweight, dumbbells, barbells, benches,
  kettlebells, resistance bands, pull-up bars, squat racks, cable and cardio
  machines
• Weight loss, muscle gain, strength, endurance or general fitness
• Injuries and problem areas are worked around, not ignored
• Choose how many days a week you train and how long each session runs

TRACK EVERY SET
• Log weight, reps and time for each set as you go
• Last week's numbers sit beside today's, so you know what to beat
• A built-in timer for planks, intervals and any timed exercise
• A video tutorial for every exercise, so you know the movement before you
  try it
• Tick off exercises as you finish them and watch the session fill in

YOUR WEEK, YOUR WAY
• Drag a session onto another weekday when life gets in the way
• Repeat a week that went well, or rewrite one that didn't
• Track completed weeks and revisit any past session
• Metric or imperial, with separate units for the plates in your gym

WHY TRAINR
Most workout apps hand you the same plan as everyone else. Trainr builds yours
from your answers, then progresses it each week from your logged sets. It is a
workout planner, a rep tracker and an exercise log in one, and your week is
built on your phone, not on a server.

PRIVACY
Your profile and training history stay on your phone. Trainr has no accounts
and no server of its own, and nothing you answer is sent anywhere.

Trainr Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time
lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew until cancelled in your Apple Account
settings. Terms of use: https://jerichomagallanes.github.io/Trainr/terms-of-use
Privacy policy: https://jerichomagallanes.github.io/Trainr/privacy-policy

Plans are built by the app and are not medical advice. Check with a doctor
before starting a new programme, and stop if something hurts.
```

[2350] Apple does not index the description, so it is written to be read.
The paragraph on Pro is there because App Review requires the product page of
an app with auto-renewable subscriptions to say they renew and to link the
terms and the privacy policy.

## Screenshots

One set is required, at the 6.9-inch size (1320 × 2868), captured on the
iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator with the status bar set to 9:41 and full battery.
Apple scales that set down for every other iPhone. Order, which is also the
order on the store page:

1. Welcome — the promise in one line.
2. Equipment setup — the question that makes the week yours.
3. Weekly plan — the week itself, with today marked.
4. A day — the card with labelled muscles, how-to and the set table.
5. Weekly progress — what the app does with what you logged.

The paywall is not in the set: prices differ by storefront and Apple compares
any price shown against the products.
